# Why Is the Sky Blue?

## A Geometric Derivation of Rayleigh Scattering from Maxwell Theory

This page hosts the LaTeX source for an article on Rayleigh scattering, Maxwell theory, and spacetime algebra.

The aim is not popularization in the usual sense, but structural clarification: to show how the familiar Rayleigh law follows from the geometrical and dynamical structure of Maxwell theory.
